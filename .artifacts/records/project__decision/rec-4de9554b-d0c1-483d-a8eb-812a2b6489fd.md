## Rationale

Finished and closed answer different questions. Finished is derived: every phase and counted task of the effort is done, so the dashboard dims it and sorts it last. Closed is the user's call that no more phases follow: the goal and position carry `status: closed` and drop out of handoff, brief and the dashboard. Closing needs no runtime change, because `status` is an optional payload field in the se-workflow contract and supersede merges it. This supersedes the premise of finished-effort-derived, not its dashboard behaviour.

## Counter-argument

Two markers for "this effort is over" can disagree: a finished effort may stay open for months, and a closed one may still have planned tasks. One derived signal is simpler to reason about.
