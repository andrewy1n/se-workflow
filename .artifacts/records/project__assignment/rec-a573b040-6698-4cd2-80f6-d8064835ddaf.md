Requirements (quoted):
R1: The phase screen replaces phase detail and has tabs Overview, Spec, Design, Decisions, Evidence, Tasks. A phase with no specification, or whose contract lacks the specification type, shows Overview, Spec, Decisions, and Tasks. `left` and `right` move between tabs; `p`, `s`, `d`, `e`, and `t` jump to Overview, Spec, Decisions, Evidence, and Tasks.
R5: Spec, for a simple phase, renders the phase body (Problem, Approach, Exit criteria, Landing) and the phase constraints.
R7: Decisions lists the phase's active decisions as rows with subject and choice; Enter expands a row to alternatives, rationale, and counter-argument. `a` toggles the whole effort, grouped under phase titles in ordinal order. `h` toggles superseded decisions, each shown dimmed under its successor with `supersedes` and its own choice.
R13: The phase screen reloads on store change, as phase detail does today, keeping the tab, toggles, and scroll position.

Design Invariants:
`PhaseScreen` reads only; the temp file is created outside the store and always removed. A missing specification or design type in the contract degrades to the simple tab set, not an error. The requirement detail and task detail screens are reused unchanged.

Design Assumptions:
Textual `TabbedContent` supports removing panes before mount and switching with `left`/`right` when the tab bar has focus; the panes bind `left`/`right` explicitly so it works from content focus too. `App.suspend()` is available in the resolved Textual version.

Decisions:
- phase-screen-tabs: Phase detail becomes one tabbed phase screen (Overview, Spec, Design, Decisions, Evidence, Tasks); p, s, and e open it at Overview, Spec, or Evidence for the selected phase, or the cursor row's phase on All phases
- spec-full-text: Spec and Design tabs show the full record text as Markdown; each requirement is a selectable row that opens its requirement detail
- decision-log-scope: The Decisions tab shows the phase's decisions by default, a toggles the whole effort grouped by phase, and h toggles superseded decisions shown under their successors
- simple-phase-spec-tab: A phase with no specification shows its phase body and constraints on the Spec tab and hides the Design and Evidence tabs
