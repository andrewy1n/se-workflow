## Requirements

R1: A work-item may carry `size` of `XS`, `S`, `M`, `L`, or `XL`. Absent means the size is unset. The meanings are: `XS` a trivial or local change, `S` a small change, `M` a moderate change, `L` a substantial or multi-file change, `XL` a large or high-risk change.
R2: A work-item may carry `estimate_minutes`, a non-negative integer, meaning the working time of that task's executor. Absent means unset. Zero is a recorded estimate. The field name is `estimate_minutes`.
R3: `plan-phase` writes `size` and `estimate_minutes` on every new work-item, choosing `size` by the R1 meanings. The minutes are for that task's executor, `inline` or `subagent`. A missing executor means the minutes are for a subagent.
R4: The phase record does not store `size` or `estimate_minutes`. Inline minutes in a wave add. A subagent wave's elapsed estimate is the longest `estimate_minutes` among its tasks. Phase elapsed time adds those wave figures in wave order. Blank estimates are skipped, and a wave whose estimates are all blank has no elapsed estimate.
R5: The phase body still records session shape as `one sitting` or `more than one`.
R6: This phase does not change what the dashboard renders.

## Acceptance criteria

R1 and R2: `contract/project-design.json` allows optional `size` (`XS`, `S`, `M`, `L`, `XL`) and optional `estimate_minutes` on `project:work-item`, and `python3 -m pytest tests/test_skill_contract_consistency.py -q` passes.
R3, R4, and R5: `skills/plan-phase/SKILL.md` requires `size` and `estimate_minutes` on each new work-item, states the five size meanings, the inline sum, the subagent-wave maximum, and the phase elapsed time, and still requires the sitting line. The same consistency test passes.
R6: the phase diff does not change `dashboard/`.

## Constraints

No change to `~/adaptive-artifacts`. Existing work-items stay valid with `size` and `estimate_minutes` absent. The dashboard stays read-only.

## Non-goals

Rendering size, `estimate_minutes`, the sitting line, or the executor. A stored minute total on the phase. Deriving `estimate_minutes` from the size, or the reverse. Replacing `one sitting` or `more than one`. Using assignee as the agent the minutes belong to. A field named `minutes` or `hours`.

## Invariants

Absent `estimate_minutes` are not zero. Size and `estimate_minutes` are stored independently. The executor is who the minutes refer to. Subagent estimates in one wave are not added together into that wave's elapsed estimate. Inline minutes and subagent minutes are not added across a wave boundary except as the sum of the wave figures in wave order.

## Assumptions

`estimate_minutes` is a whole number. Tasks in one wave already share one executor. Wave membership stays the derived `wave` on the work-item. `plan-phase` is the writer.
