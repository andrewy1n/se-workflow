## Requirements

R1: A work-item may carry `size` of `S`, `M`, or `L`. Absent means the size is unset.
R2: A work-item may carry `minutes`, a non-negative integer, meaning the working time of that task's executor. Absent means unset. Zero is a recorded estimate.
R3: `plan-phase` writes `size` and `minutes` on every new work-item. The minutes are for that task's executor, `inline` or `subagent`. A missing executor means the minutes are for a subagent.
R4: The phase record does not store `size` or `minutes`. Inline minutes in a wave add. A subagent wave's elapsed estimate is the longest `minutes` among its tasks. Phase elapsed time adds those wave figures in wave order. Blank minutes are skipped, and a wave whose minutes are all blank has no elapsed estimate.
R5: The phase body still records session shape as `one sitting` or `more than one`.
R6: This phase does not change what the dashboard renders.

## Acceptance criteria

R1 and R2: `contract/project-design.json` allows optional `size` (`S`, `M`, `L`) and optional `minutes` on `project:work-item`, and `python3 -m pytest tests/test_skill_contract_consistency.py -q` passes.
R3, R4, and R5: `skills/plan-phase/SKILL.md` requires `size` and `minutes` on each new work-item, states the inline sum, the subagent-wave maximum, and the phase elapsed time, and still requires the sitting line. The same consistency test passes.
R6: the phase diff does not change `dashboard/`.

## Constraints

No change to `~/adaptive-artifacts`. Existing work-items stay valid with `size` and `minutes` absent. The dashboard stays read-only.

## Non-goals

Rendering size, minutes, the sitting line, or the executor. A stored minute total on the phase. Deriving minutes from S, M, L, or the reverse. Replacing `one sitting` or `more than one`. Using assignee as the agent the minutes belong to. An hours field.

## Invariants

Absent minutes are not zero. Size and minutes are stored independently. The executor is who the minutes refer to. Subagent minutes in one wave are not added together into that wave's elapsed estimate. Inline minutes and subagent minutes are not added across a wave boundary except as the sum of the wave figures in wave order.

## Assumptions

`minutes` is a whole number. Tasks in one wave already share one executor. Wave membership stays the derived `wave` on the work-item. `plan-phase` is the writer.
