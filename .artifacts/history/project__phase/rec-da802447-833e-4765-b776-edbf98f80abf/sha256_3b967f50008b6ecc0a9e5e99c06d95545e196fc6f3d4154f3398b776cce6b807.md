## Problem

Engage created a new effort for each ask. Four dashboard efforts ended up side by side, and the effort list looked scattered.

## Approach

Treat an effort as a product area. Engage compares the ask with live goals and reuses a matching effort, adding a new phase. init needs a reason for a new subject. plan-phase creates a `phase: new` with the next ordinal.

## Exit criteria

engage, init, kinds-and-focus and plan-phase carry the rule. Committed at 56531c8 on feat/effort-phases.
