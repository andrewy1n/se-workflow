## Problem

A task has no record of how big it is or how long the agent who will do it should take. The phase body records session shape as `one sitting` or `more than one`, and the work-item records `executor` as `inline` or `subagent`. Neither is a duration. The contract has no `size` or `estimate_minutes` field, so those values cannot be stored yet.

## Approach

Decisions: task-agent-estimate, glance-run-facts. Run as structured work: specification `run-facts`, design `run-facts`.

Each new work-item stores `size` and `estimate_minutes` for its executor. `size` is `XS` a trivial or local change, `S` a small change, `M` a moderate change, `L` a substantial or multi-file change, or `XL` a large or high-risk change. `estimate_minutes` is a non-negative integer. Absent means unset, and zero is a real estimate. `plan-phase` writes both with the executor. A missing executor means the minutes are for a subagent. Size and `estimate_minutes` are not derived from each other.

The phase record does not store either field. Inline minutes in a wave add. A subagent wave's elapsed estimate is its longest task. Phase elapsed time adds those wave figures in wave order. Blank estimates are skipped. The sitting line stays `one sitting` or `more than one`.

Dashboard display waits for a later phase. This phase does not render size, `estimate_minutes`, the sitting line, or the executor, and it does not edit `dashboard/` or `~/adaptive-artifacts`.

Size: one sitting. Human stops: plan review. No push.

Waves:

1. `estimate-contract` and `estimate-skill` (subagent, parallel). `estimate-contract` owns `contract/project-design.json`, `.artifacts/project-design.json`, and `tests/test_estimate_contract.py`. `estimate-skill` owns `skills/plan-phase/SKILL.md` and `tests/test_estimate_skill.py`.
2. `verify-run-facts` (subagent) owns `tests/test_estimate_cli.py` and runs the phase checks after wave 1.

Collision notes: wave 1 shares no written file. Neither task edits `dashboard/`.
