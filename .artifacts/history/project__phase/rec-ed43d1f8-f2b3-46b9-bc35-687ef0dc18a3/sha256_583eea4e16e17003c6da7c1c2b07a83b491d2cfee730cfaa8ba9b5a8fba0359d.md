## Problem

What was learned while working has no home on the dashboard. Failed attempts, investigation observations, findings, continuity questions, and assignment amendments appear only when one lands in Needs you, so a reader resuming an effort cannot see what was tried, what was observed, or what not to retry. Repair and evaluate efforts are mostly this story, but they open on the same task table as delivery work. The task detail shows that a task was assigned and reported, but not what the executor was told or what it returned.

## Approach

Decisions: journal-screen, journal-order, journal-types, kind-journal-panel, task-agent-work. Run as structured work: specification `journal-view`, design `journal-view`.

`J` on the main screen opens a Journal screen for the selected phase, and `a` toggles the whole effort. It lists failed attempts (marked don't retry), observations, findings, continuity questions, and assignment amendments. Open items come first: open questions, findings that need a human, and observations with no finding yet. Everything else follows, newest first. Repair and evaluate efforts show a Journal panel of their open items above the task table, and delivery efforts are unchanged. The task detail adds the latest assignment, its amendments, and its execution report, each collapsed to 8 lines, with Enter to expand and `o` for the pager. Older assignments are listed by time.

This phase edits the dashboard and does not change `contract/project-design.json` or `~/adaptive-artifacts`. It starts after `spec-view` is on main, because it reuses the pager and the phase-or-effort toggle. It can run beside `evidence-matrix`: the two share only `dashboard/model.py` loaders, and that is the collision area.

## Exit criteria

- Item selection per type, phase scoping, open-first ordering, the kind panel rule, and the assignment and report bodies hold against seeded stores (`tests/test_dashboard_model.py`, `tests/test_dashboard_journal.py`).
- `J`, `a`, Enter, Esc, collapsed and expanded agent sections, and `o` with the pager stubbed behave as specified in a Textual pilot (`tests/test_dashboard_journal.py`, `tests/test_dashboard_app.py`).
- The full suite passes.
