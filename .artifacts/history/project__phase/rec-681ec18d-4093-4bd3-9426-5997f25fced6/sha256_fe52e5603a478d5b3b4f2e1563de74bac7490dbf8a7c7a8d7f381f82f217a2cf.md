## Problem

Phase detail is one long scroll of nine panels, and it never shows the specification or design text: requirement wording, acceptance criteria, invariants, architecture, risks. Decisions show only their one-line choice, without rationale, alternatives, or what they superseded, and only for one phase. Long text is hard to read in a 40%-wide side pane, and the dashboard offers no way to read it elsewhere.

## Approach

Decisions: phase-screen-tabs, spec-full-text, decision-log-scope, pager-temp-file, simple-phase-spec-tab. Run as structured work: specification `spec-view`, design `spec-view`.

Phase detail becomes one tabbed phase screen: Overview, Spec, Design, Decisions, Evidence, Tasks. `p`, `s`, and `e` open it at Overview, Spec, or Evidence. Spec and Design show full Markdown; Decisions toggle effort scope and superseded history; `o` pages the current tab via a temp file outside the store. A simple phase shows phase body and constraints on Spec and hides Design and Evidence.

Tasks (all `executor: subagent`):

1. `spec-model` — extend `load_phase_detail` with spec/design bodies, `tabs_for`, and `load_decision_log`. Cover with `tests/test_dashboard_model.py` (`-k phase_screen`).
2. `spec-pager` — add `pager_command` and temp-file helpers outside the store. Cover with a stubbed pager unit/pilot helper test.
3. `spec-screen` — replace phase detail with `PhaseScreen` panes, main-screen `p`/`s`/`e`, `[`/`]`, Esc, and `o`. Depends on model and pager. Cover with `tests/test_dashboard_phase_screen.py`.
4. `verify-spec-view` — integration and full suite.

Collision notes: `dashboard/app.py` and phase-detail extraction belong to `spec-screen`. `dashboard/model.py` loaders belong to `spec-model`. Pager helpers are a new small module (or beside phase_screen) owned by `spec-pager` until `spec-screen` wires `o`.

Size: more than one sitting. Human stops: plan review; then landing after verification. Execute still waits on the other UX phases being planned (constraint `dashboard-ux-plan-before-execute`).

## Exit criteria

- Tab sets for structured and simple phases, decision log scopes, and superseded links hold against seeded stores (`tests/test_dashboard_model.py`, `tests/test_dashboard_phase_screen.py`).
- Keys, requirement links, Esc returning to the same tab, and `o` with the pager stubbed behave as specified in a Textual pilot (`tests/test_dashboard_phase_screen.py`).
- The full suite passes.
