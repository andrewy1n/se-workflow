## Architecture

`contract/project-design.json` adds optional `size` and `hours` to `project:work-item`. `skills/plan-phase/SKILL.md` requires both on every new work-item and states how a reader totals them. Nothing in `dashboard/` changes. The sitting line stays prose in the phase body.

A later phase can render these fields. This phase's job ends when the contract accepts them and `plan-phase` is required to write them.

## Interfaces

- Work-item payload `size`, optional, enum `S`, `M`, `L`.
- Work-item payload `hours`, optional, a non-negative number.
- `plan-phase` sets both in the same payload as `executor`.
- Derived, not stored:
  - An inline wave's elapsed hours are the sum of its tasks' `hours`, skipping blanks.
  - A subagent wave's elapsed hours are the maximum of its tasks' `hours`, skipping blanks.
  - Phase elapsed hours are those wave figures added in wave order, skipping a wave that has no hours.
  - A size tally is the count of `S`, `M`, and `L` on the phase's tasks.
- The phase payload stays `title`, `ordinal`, and `effort`.

## Decisions

glance-run-facts, task-agent-estimate.

## Invariants

Absent `hours` are not zero. `size` and `hours` are not derived from each other. The sitting line remains `one sitting` or `more than one`. Hours refer to `payload.executor`, and a missing executor means subagent. Assignee is not the rollup key.

## Assumptions

`hours` may be fractional. A wave's tasks share one executor. The derived `wave` is the grouping. Old work-items have neither field until a later edit, and totals skip them.

## Tradeoffs

There is no single stored phase total, and phase elapsed time is not the sum of every task. Parallel subagent work counts once, as the longest task. S, M, and L can disagree with the hours, and both stay so the disagreement is visible. Loading nothing new into the dashboard keeps this phase off the files `evidence-view` is editing.

## Risks

Hours will be guessed. A partial total, when some tasks omit hours, can be read as the whole phase. The later display phase has to use the wave rule; summing every task's hours would contradict this decision. `evidence-view` is still in progress, so this phase stays away from `dashboard/` until that branch is on main.
