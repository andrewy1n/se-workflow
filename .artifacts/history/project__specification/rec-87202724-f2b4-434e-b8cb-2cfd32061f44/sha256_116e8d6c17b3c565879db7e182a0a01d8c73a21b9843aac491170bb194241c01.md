## Requirements

R1: A work-item may carry `size` of `S`, `M`, or `L`. Absent means the size is unset.
R2: A work-item may carry `hours`, a non-negative number, meaning the working time of that task's executor. Absent means unset. Zero is a recorded estimate.
R3: `plan-phase` writes `size` and `hours` on every new work-item. The hours are for that task's executor, `inline` or `subagent`. A missing executor means the hours are for a subagent.
R4: The phase record does not store `size` or `hours`. Inline hours in a wave add. A subagent wave's elapsed estimate is the longest `hours` among its tasks. Phase elapsed time adds those wave figures in wave order. Blank hours are skipped, and a wave whose hours are all blank has no elapsed estimate.
R5: The phase body still records session shape as `one sitting` or `more than one`.
R6: This phase does not change what the dashboard renders.

## Acceptance criteria

R1 and R2: `contract/project-design.json` allows optional `size` (`S`, `M`, `L`) and optional `hours` on `project:work-item`, and `python3 -m pytest tests/test_skill_contract_consistency.py -q` passes.
R3, R4, and R5: `skills/plan-phase/SKILL.md` requires `size` and `hours` on each new work-item, states the inline sum, the subagent-wave maximum, and the phase elapsed time, and still requires the sitting line. The same consistency test passes.
R6: the phase diff does not change `dashboard/`.

## Constraints

No change to `~/adaptive-artifacts`. Existing work-items stay valid with `size` and `hours` absent. The dashboard stays read-only.

## Non-goals

Rendering size, hours, the sitting line, or the executor. A stored hour total on the phase. Deriving hours from S, M, L, or the reverse. Replacing `one sitting` or `more than one`. Using assignee as the agent the hours belong to.

## Invariants

Absent hours are not zero. Size and hours are stored independently. The executor is who the hours refer to. Subagent hours in one wave are not added together into that wave's elapsed estimate. Inline hours and subagent hours are not added across a wave boundary except as the sum of the wave figures in wave order.

## Assumptions

`hours` may be fractional. Tasks in one wave already share one executor. Wave membership stays the derived `wave` on the work-item. `plan-phase` is the writer.
