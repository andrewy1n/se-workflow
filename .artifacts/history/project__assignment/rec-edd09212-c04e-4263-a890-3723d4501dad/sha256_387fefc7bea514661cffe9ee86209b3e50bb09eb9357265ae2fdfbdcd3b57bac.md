## Requirements

R1: A phase header, after the done/total count, shows `one sitting` or `more than one` when the phase body has a matching `Size:` line. `Size: more than one sitting` shows as `more than one`. A body with no Size line shows no sitting phrase.
R2: That header then shows the phase elapsed minutes as `{n}m`. Elapsed minutes follow the wave rule: inline minutes in a wave add, a subagent wave contributes its longest `estimate_minutes`, and those wave figures add in wave order. Blank estimates are skipped. A wave with no estimates contributes nothing. The header does not show the sum of every task's minutes.
R3: When one or more counted tasks have no `estimate_minutes` and at least one does, the header keeps the partial `{n}m` and appends `{k} unset`, where k is how many counted tasks lack an estimate. When every counted task has an estimate, there is no unset count. When none have an estimate, the header has no minute suffix and no unset count.
R4: Each task title, in a dim suffix after the title and before a waits-on note, shows `size` when set, then `{n}m` when `estimate_minutes` is set, then `inline` or `subagent`. A missing or empty executor shows as `subagent`. A missing size or a missing estimate is omitted. Zero minutes show as `0m`.
R5: Clipping shortens the title and keeps the sitting phrase, the elapsed minutes, the unset count, and the task suffix.
R6: Task detail shows chips for the size when set, `{n}m` when set, and `executor inline` or `executor subagent`.
R7: A task with no `size` and no `estimate_minutes` still shows its executor. This phase does not change `contract/project-design.json`.

## Invariants

Header minutes are the wave rule, not the sum of every task. A missing estimate is omitted on the task and counted as unset on the phase. A missing size is omitted. A missing executor is `subagent`. Suffixes survive clipping. The dashboard stays read-only.

## Decisions

estimate-titles: Show the sitting phrase and derived elapsed minutes on the phase title, and size, estimate_minutes, and executor on the task title. Task detail repeats them as chips. A partial estimate shows the minute total and an unset count.

task-agent-estimate: Each work-item stores size XS, S, M, L, or XL and estimate_minutes for its own executor. Inline minutes in a wave add. A subagent wave elapsed estimate is its longest task. Phase elapsed time adds those wave figures in wave order. The phase stores neither field.

glance-run-facts: Defer dashboard display until size and estimate_minutes are stored. A later phase shows the sitting line and executor as title suffixes.
