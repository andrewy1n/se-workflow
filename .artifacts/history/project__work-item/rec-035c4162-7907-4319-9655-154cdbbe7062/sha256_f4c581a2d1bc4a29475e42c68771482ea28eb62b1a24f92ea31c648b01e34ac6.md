## Description

Require plan-phase to write size and estimate_minutes on every new work-item, and state how a reader totals the minutes. The phase body still records sitting shape.

## Approach

Read specification `run-facts` requirements R3, R4, and R5, and decisions task-agent-estimate and glance-run-facts. Edit only `skills/plan-phase/SKILL.md` and `tests/test_estimate_skill.py`. Do not edit `contract/`, `dashboard/`, `skills/kinds-and-focus.md`, `README.md`, or `AGENTS.md`.

Keep the sentences the existing tests require, including `size as `one sitting` or `more than one``, `Every task in a wave uses the same executor.`, and `Missing `executor` means `subagent``. Every example work-item payload keeps `"executor": "subagent"`. Keep the trace-field rules.

Add, next to the executor rules:

- Every new work-item payload includes `size` and `estimate_minutes`.
- `size` is `XS` a trivial or local change, `S` a small change, `M` a moderate change, `L` a substantial or multi-file change, or `XL` a large or high-risk change.
- `estimate_minutes` is a non-negative integer for that task's executor. Absent means unset. Zero is a real estimate. A missing executor means the minutes are for a subagent.
- `size` and `estimate_minutes` are not derived from each other. The phase record stores neither field.
- Inline minutes in a wave add. A subagent wave's elapsed estimate is the longest `estimate_minutes` among its tasks. Phase elapsed time adds those wave figures in wave order. Blank estimates are skipped. A wave whose estimates are all blank has no elapsed estimate.
- The phase body still records session shape as `one sitting` or `more than one`.

Put `"size": "M"` and `"estimate_minutes": 25` on every work-item payload in the skill's capture and create examples, including the verification task. `25` is a JSON number.

Write `tests/test_estimate_skill.py` first. Parse the skill with `skill_extraction.extract_invocations`. Assert every work-item payload has a size in that enum and an integer `estimate_minutes` of at least zero, and that the flat skill text contains the five meanings, the inline sum, the subagent-wave maximum, the phase elapsed time, and `one sitting`.
