## Description

Add the evidence-loop record types and the trace fields the later skills write.

## Approach

Edit `contract/project-design.json`. Copy it to `.artifacts/project-design.json` and run `adaptive-artifacts resolve`. Do not edit the adaptive-artifacts runtime.

Add these records on existing patterns:

- `specification` (definition). Payload `weight`, `phase`, `effort`, `requirements`. `weight` enum `light` | `full`. `phase` references `project:phase`. Required sections: Requirements, Acceptance criteria, Constraints, Non-goals, Invariants, Assumptions.
- `design` (definition). Payload `phase`, `effort`, `decisions`. `phase` references `project:phase`. Required sections: Architecture, Interfaces, Decisions, Invariants, Assumptions, Tradeoffs, Risks.
- `integration-report` (observation). Payload `phase`, `effort`, `result`, `revision`. `result` enum `pass` | `fail` | `blocked`. Required sections: Interfaces, Assumptions, Invariants, Conflicts.
- `assessment` (current-status). Payload `requirement`, `status`, `level`, `next`, `confidence`, `phase`, `effort`, `missing`. Optional: `level`, `missing`. Enums: `status` `verified` | `failed` | `insufficient` | `blocked`; `level` `implementation` | `design` | `plan` | `specification` | `integration` | `verification` | `environment` | `unknown`; `next` `specify` | `design` | `plan` | `execute` | `integrate` | `verify` | `release`; `confidence` `high` | `low`. Required sections: Evidence, Missing, Reason. Purpose: given the check-runs, what the requirement's evidence means and which stage is next. A check-run does not carry this.
- `release` (current-status). Payload `state`, `phase`, `effort`. `state` enum `ready` | `merged` | `artifact` | `deploying` | `deployed` | `failed`. Purpose: `ready` means every requirement is verified. It is not deployment. The other states exist for a later phase.
- `feedback` (observation). Payload `source`, `signal`, `effort`, `phase`. Optional: `phase`. Required section: Signal.

Extend existing records. Keep every new field optional so current records still validate:

- `acceptance`: optional `requirement`.
- `work-item`: optional `requirements` and `decisions` (leave `executor` optional).
- `check-run`: optional `requirement` and `evidence_kind`. `result` enum `pass` | `fail` | `blocked` | `insufficient`. `evidence_kind` enum `unit` | `integration` | `acceptance` | `static` | `types` | `security` | `property` | `fuzz` | `review` | `runtime` | `other`. Do not add `level` or `next` to check-run.

In `skills/ensure-store.md`, extend the first-run upgrade check so a design is current only when it also lists `specification`, `design`, `assessment`, `integration-report`, `release`, and `feedback`.

Do not edit skills other than `ensure-store.md`, and do not edit `dashboard/`.
