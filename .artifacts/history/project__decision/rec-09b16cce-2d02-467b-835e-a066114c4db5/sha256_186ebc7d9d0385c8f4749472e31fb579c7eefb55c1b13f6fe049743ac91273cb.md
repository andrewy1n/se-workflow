## Rationale

`n` is free in every screen, matches the panel name, and keeps tab/shift+tab for effort switching. One key from anywhere on the main screen beats walking past the last task row.

## Counter-argument

A dedicated key is one more thing to learn; moving down past the last task row into the list is more discoverable and needs no footer space.
