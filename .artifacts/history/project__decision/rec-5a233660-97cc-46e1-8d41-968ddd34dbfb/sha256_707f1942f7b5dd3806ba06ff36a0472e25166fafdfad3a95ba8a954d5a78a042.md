## Rationale

The phase body and the work-item payload already hold these facts, and the pane is 60 columns. A new column disappears below 90 columns, the same way assignee does. Putting size on the phase title and executor on the task title keeps both visible in the scan, and the task-detail chip states the executor next to assignee. Parsing the Size line avoids a second copy that can drift from the prose `plan-phase` is required to write.

## Counter-argument

A `size` field on the phase would be a stable enum, and the body wording has already drifted between `more than one` and `more than one sitting`. A parser can miss a line a field would not, and every existing phase stays blank until something copies the line across.
